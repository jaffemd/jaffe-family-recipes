1. **Preheat the oven**: Know your oven. The goal here is to get the vegetables nice and roasted, almost charred but not burnt. Set your oven to a high temperature where that will happen. For our oven that's 450 degrees on convection, but that might be different on yours.

2. **Choose your vegetables**: Choose a mix of vegetables that will give you a mix of good colors and flavors.

3. **Prepare vegetable**: Peel, chop, slice, dice your vegetetables until they're all around the same size. 

4. **Season**: In a bowl or on a sheet pan, mix with a generous amount of olive oil, salt, pepper and your favorite spices or herbs. 

5. **Spread evenly**: Spread all vegetables on an unlined sheet pan. They should be spread out enough so that they're not overcrowded and each piece is touching the bottom of the pan. 

6. **Roast**: Roast for 20-30 minutes, until all vegetables are tender and they're starting to get charred but not yet burnt. Take the pan out and shuckle them around every 7-10 minute.s
