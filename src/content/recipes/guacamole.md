---
title: "Guacamole"
description: "Fresh guacamole made with ripe avocados, lime, cilantro, and just the right amount of heat. Perfect as a dip for chips at a party or family gathering."
blurb: "Easy party dish and a crowd favorite."
prepTime: "15 minutes"
cookTime: "0"
totalTime: "15 minutes"
servings: "4"
ingredients:
  - "2 ripe avocados"
  - "1/2 yellow or white onion"
  - "1 clove garlic"
  - "Juice from 1 lime"
  - "1/2 jalapeno or other spicy pepper (optional)"
  - "1/4 cup or so chopped cilantro"
  - "1 tsp salt"
tags:
  - "dip"
  - "party"
---

1. **Smoosh Avocado**: Peel or scoop out the meat of the avocados and discard the pit. Smoosh with a potato masher or fork until it's smooth but still slightly chunky.

2. **Chop other stuff**: Finely dice the onion. Finely dice or press the garlic. Deseed and finely dice the jalapeno. Chop the cliantro leaves and stems. 

3. **Combine**: Combine all ingredients and mix with a fork. Taste with a chip and add more salt or lime if needed. 

4. **Serve**: Serve immediately or cover with plastic wrap and store in the fridge for a couple hours at most. The lime juice and plastic wrap should keep it from getting too brown.
