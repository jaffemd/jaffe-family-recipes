prepTime: "30 minutes"

servings: "4-6"
ingredients:
  - "3 pound brisket"
  - "1/2 cup ketchup"
  - "1 quart of chicken stock"
  - "1/4 cup worcestershire sauce"
  - "1/4 cup soy sauce"
  - "hot sauce to taste"
  - "A lot of garlic"  
  - "1 onion, sliced"
  - "A couple carrots, peeled and sliced"
  - "1 TBSP smoked paprika"
  - "salt and pepper"

1. **Prepare vegetables**: Slice the carrots and onion. Mince or press the garlic and add it to the bottom of the slow cooker. 

2. **Season and sear brisket**: Season the brisket on both sides and sear it on high heat in a pan until both sides are brown. Add it to the slow cooker.

3. **Comnbine liquid ingredients**: Combine all liquid ingredients in a bowl and stir to combine. Add them to the slow cooker.

4. **Start the slow cooker**: Set the slow cooker to high for 4 hours, or low for 8 hours. Start checking the brisket after 75% of the time. We're looking for it to be tender throughout, but not so tender that it completely falls apart.

5. **Rest and slice**: When it's done, set it on a cutting board for 15 to 30 minutes to rest. Then slice it and return it to the pot with the sauce to serve and keep warm.
