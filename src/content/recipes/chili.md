ingredients:
  - "2 pounds of meat or meat substitute"
  - "1 28oz can of san marzano tomatoes"
  - "1 quart of chicken or vegetable stock"
  - "1 large onion"
  - "1 can of beans, drained and rinsed"
  - "A lot of garlic"  
  - "a bunch of salt to taste"
  - "2 TBSP of cumin"
  - "1 TBSP smoked paprika"
  - "1 tsp to several TBSP of chipotle chili powder"
  - "1 tsp to several TBSP of ancho chili powder"
  - "1 tsp ground cinnamon"
  - "1 TBSP Worcestershire sauce"
  - "1/2 TBSP Worcestershire sauce"
  - "whatever other spices you have in your pantry that you want to try"

1. **Saute meat**: On high heat, add some oil add the meat and saute on high heat for 2-3 minutes until you see some color. 

2. **Saute other stuff**: Add in the garlic, onion and all the spices and sautee for another 2-3 minutes. 

3. **Choose your cooking vessel**: Now you have a choice, you can use any of the following to cook chili:
- A dutch oven
- A stock pot
- A slow cooker
- Anything else that can hold a large amount of liquid and maintain a low simmer. 

4. **Combine ingredients**: Add your sauteed ingredients, plus the rest of the ingredients from the ingredient list to the your cooking vessel excet for the beans.

5. **Simmer**: Simmer or slow cook for a couple hours. Taste periodically and add more seasoning, spices or liquid if needed. The chili is cooked through almost immeidately, but the longer it cooks, the better it will taste. If using stew meat, cook until the meat is tender. 

6. **Beans**: Add the beans during the last 30-60 minutes so they don't become too mushy (unless you like that). 

7. **Accompaniments**: Serve with copious toppings such as cheddar cheese, hot sauce, avocado, green onion and/or sour cream.

8. **Serve**: Serve with cornbread (mandatory). Eat leftovers the next day on top of macaroni noodles.
