1. **Preheat the oven**: Preheat a convection oven to 450 degrees.

2. **Saute onion and garlic**: Saute the onion and garlic in olive oil until they're translucent, about 5 minutes.

3. **Combine ingredients**: In a large bowl, combine all the ingredients except for the tomato sauce.

4. **Cook a tester**: You'll probably have some leftover mixture stuck to your hands. Form that into a small patty and cook that in the same pan you sauteed the onions in for a couple minutes until cooked through. This let's you test the seasoning and adjust if needed.

5. **Prepare the pan**: Line a large sheetpan with non stick aluminum foil (makes clean up easier, but this step is optional). Generously drizzle olive oil on the bottom of the pan.

6. **Form the balls**: Using an ice cream scoop, any other spoon you have, or your hands, form the meatballs into whatever size you like. We prefer smaller balls, about the size of a golf ball. They have to be perfectly ball shaped, anything close will do.

7. **Cook the balls**: Put the sheet pan on the top rack of the oven. Cook the balls for 10-15 minutes total. Flip them halfway through. The outsides should be golden brown, and the inside should be completely cooked (165 degrees), but not dried out.

8. **Simmer in sauce**: This step is optional. If serving with tomato sauce, simmer the balls in the sauce on a low to medium heat for 5-10 minutes.

## Flexibility

This recipe works well with almost any number of substitutions. Pretty much any ground meat will work here, the accompanying ingredients will ensure it stays moist and flavorful. It's also been heavily stress tested with gluten free and egg free ingredients, and still works great.
